QuickMart Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

QuickMart ("we", "our", or "us") operates the QuickMart hyperlocal grocery ordering and delivery platform. We are dedicated to maintaining the privacy and security of your personal data. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning data collection, usage, sharing, retention, and deletion.

1. Personal Information We Collect

  • Mobile Phone Number: Required for secure SMS OTP authentication, order confirmation, and delivery coordination.
  • Customer Name: Stored to identify the customer during order handoff.
  • Delivery Addresses: House numbers, street/area names, village/town names, and delivery instructions to dispatch grocery orders to your doorstep.
  • Geographical Coordinates (Location Data): Collected only with your explicit foreground permission while using the app to calculate distance from our local distribution hub, determine deliverability, and compute accurate distance-based delivery fees.
  • Order History: Details of grocery items ordered, quantities, subtotal, delivery fee, order timestamps, and Cash on Delivery (COD) payment status.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate customer accounts via one-time passwords (OTP).
  • To compute accurate, distance-based delivery fees and delivery time estimates.
  • To process, fulfill, and deliver orders to your delivery address.
  • To verify Cash on Delivery transactions via a 2-step verification code.
  • To comply with legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

3. Location Data Usage

QuickMart accesses your precise or approximate location only while the application is active in the foreground. Location data is strictly used to check service availability within our delivery radius (15 km) and compute distance-based delivery fees. We do not track location in the background, nor do we sell or share location data with third-party advertising networks.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We work only with essential service providers necessary to operate the application:

  • SMS Gateways (APITXT): To transmit one-time authentication and order verification passwords via SMS.
  • Cloud Infrastructure & Database (MongoDB Atlas, Cloud Hosting): For secure and encrypted database storage.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your profile data as long as your account remains active. Customers have full control over their personal information and may delete their account at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Navigate to Profile → Delete Account. Verify identity using a one-time OTP to finalize deletion.
  • Web Deletion Request: Visit our Account Deletion Page to request deletion of your account and personal information online.

Upon account deletion, all personal identification data (name, mobile number, saved addresses, profile pictures) are permanently deleted or anonymized. Past order transaction records are retained in anonymized format strictly as required by commercial tax and auditing laws.

6. Security

All client-server communications are encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS. Passwords and authentication tokens use cryptographic hashing and signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT). We enforce rate-limiting on sensitive operations to prevent unauthorized abuse.
