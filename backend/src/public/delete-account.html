Google Play Account Deletion Portal

Delete Your QuickMart Account

In compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, QuickMart customers can request the permanent deletion of their account and associated data directly through this portal or inside the QuickMart Android app.

What happens when you delete your account?

  • Data that is permanently deleted: Your customer name, mobile phone number, saved delivery addresses, profile photo, and active shopping cart.
  • Data retained for legitimate legal/business reasons: Past completed order receipts and invoice transaction records are archived in anonymized form for financial auditing, tax reporting, and commercial statutory compliance as mandated by law.
  • Deletion timeline: Once identity verification is completed via SMS OTP, personal profile records are deleted immediately.
